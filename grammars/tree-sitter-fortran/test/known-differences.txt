# Fixed form. The old grammar read every file as free form: column-1 C/c/*/D
# comment lines, column-6 continuation marks and the 72-column limit all came out
# as ERROR. This grammar's scanner detects fixed form from the text.

*.F      # fixed form, read as free form by the old grammar
*.for    # fixed form, read as free form by the old grammar
*.FOR    # fixed form, read as free form by the old grammar
*.ftn    # fixed form, read as free form by the old grammar
