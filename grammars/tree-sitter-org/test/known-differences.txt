# Corpus files whose trees differ from the vendored tree-sitter-org's,
# reviewed, where this grammar is right and the old one wrong. Org has no
# oracle to arbitrate (see README.md).
# Format: <path glob>  # why.  Read by tools/ts_grammar_diff (grammar-diff).

# A `# comment` line right after a directive: the old grammar read the
# comment's first line as the text of a paragraph owning the directive,
# and only the lines after it as a comment. A line starting with `# ` is a
# comment (org-syntax: Comments), so here the directive is the document's
# and the lines after it one comment.
*/doc/doc-setupfile.org                              # `# Usage:` after `#+macro:`
*/test/site/content-org/citation-org-ref.org         # `# Local Variables:` after `#+bind:`
*/test/site/content-org/issues/issue-542.org         # `# Local Variables:` after `#+bind:`
*/test/site/content-org/issues/issue-556.org         # `# Local Variables:` after `#+bind:`
