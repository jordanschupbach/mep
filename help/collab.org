* Joining a session

Each participant opens the same document, then:

#+begin_example
:CollabJoin <link> [display-name]
#+end_example

| Command          | Does                             |
|------------------+----------------------------------|
| =:CollabJoin=    | join a session                   |
| =:CollabStatus=  | who is connected                 |
| =:CollabLeave=   | disconnect                       |

* Seeing each other

Connected participants appear as chips on the [[file:status-line.html][status line]], showing name
and cursor position -- =Ada @12:4=. Click a chip to jump to that person's
cursor.

=mep.participants()= gives Lua the same snapshot, which is also what the
tab bar draws from.

* How it works

Edits are merged as CRDT operations, so two people typing in the same place
converge rather than conflicting.

The relay is *stateless by design*: it keeps transient rooms and presence
only, and forwards opaque operations without inspecting document contents.

That has one practical consequence worth planning around: *the document's
history lives in the participants, not the server.* Keeping the document
open in at least one participant preserves it. A reconnecting client
exchanges operation snapshots to catch up.

* The link is the credential

Treat a capability link as private. Anyone holding it can join that room.
