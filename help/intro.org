* Welcome

mep is a modal editor. It opens with an empty buffer and expects you to press
=i= before you can type; everything else follows from that one idea.

It is also, by now, a good deal more than a text editor: it renders and edits
Org and Markdown, PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, Jupyter notebooks,
images, 3D models and web pages, each in an ordinary pane, and it has its own
browser, its own Lua scripting layer, and its own agent protocol.

This is the manual. The sidebar on the left groups every page by topic; click
a page to read it, or press =f= to label every link on screen and jump to one
by typing its letter.

* Finding your way around

The manual runs to a hundred pages, so there are three ways in.

| Key            | Does                                                  |
|----------------+-------------------------------------------------------|
| =<leader>hh=   | open the page for whatever you are looking at         |
| =<leader>hf=   | search every page title and heading                   |
| =:help <topic>= | jump straight to a page by name                      |

=<leader>hh= is contextual: press it in a notebook and you get the notebook
page, in an R file the R mode page, in a 3D scene the modeler page. With
nothing in particular open it brings you here.

Inside a page, =f= labels every visible link so you can follow one by typing
its letter.
