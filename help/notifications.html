Notifications

Toasts

Most things mep wants to tell you arrive as a toast in the corner: a git push finishing, a formatter failing, a language server attaching. Toasts dismiss themselves after a few seconds, the more serious ones sticking around longer.

LevelDismisses after
error8 seconds
warn6 seconds
info4 seconds
debug3 seconds

When you missed one

That is what the history panel is for. <leader>nn (:MepNotifyPanel, or the Notifications button in the tab bar) lists everything that has been raised this session, including toasts that have already faded.

CommandDoes
:MepNotifyPanelopen the history panel
:MepNotifyDismissclear the toasts currently on screen
:MepNotifyClearempty the history

:MepNotifyDismiss is the one you want when a stack of toasts is covering what you are reading; it leaves the history intact.

From Lua

lua Copy
mep.notify('Build finished')
mep.notify('No formatter for this filetype', 'warn')
mep.notify('Could not reach the server', 'error')

Lua's own print() is routed through mep.notify as well, so a print in your config or in a :lua one-liner shows up as a toast rather than vanishing into a terminal mep does not have.

lua Copy
:lua print(mep.line_count())

That makes print a perfectly good debugging tool here -- and it means a chatty print in a frame hook will bury you in toasts, so keep those out of mep.on_frame.
