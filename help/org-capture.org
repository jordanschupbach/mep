* Getting a thought out of your head

=:MepOrgCapture= opens a small buffer, you write, and the result lands in
the right place in the right file -- without you navigating there and losing
what you were doing.

| Command                   | Does                       |
|---------------------------+----------------------------|
| =:MepOrgCapture=          | start a capture            |
| =:MepOrgCaptureCommit=    | file it                    |
| =:MepOrgCaptureAbort=     | throw it away              |
| =:MepOrgTemplate=         | insert a template          |

* Templates

A template says where a capture goes and what shape it has. Expansion fills
in the parts that depend on the moment -- the date, the file you were in,
the cursor's location -- so a captured note carries its context.

=mep.org_capture_expand_template(text)= is the expansion, callable directly
if you are building something of your own.
