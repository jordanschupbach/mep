Timestamps and scheduling

Inserting a date

CommandInserts
:MepOrgTimestampan active timestamp
:MepOrgTimestampInactivean inactive one, in square brackets
:MepOrgTimestampRangea range between two dates
:MepOrgTimestampRepeateradd a repeater to a timestamp

Active timestamps appear in the agenda; inactive ones do not. Use an inactive one to record when something happened without it turning into an appointment.

Adjusting one

CommandDoes
:MepOrgTimestampIncrmove the date forward
:MepOrgTimestampDecrmove it back
:MepOrgTimestampHighlighthighlight timestamps in view

Scheduling a task

CommandSets
:MepOrgScheduledSCHEDULED: -- when you intend to start
:MepOrgDeadlineDEADLINE: -- when it must be done

The distinction is worth keeping: scheduled controls when a task starts showing up in your agenda, deadline controls when it becomes urgent.
