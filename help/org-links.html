Links, footnotes and citations

Links

CommandDoes
:MepOrgLinkInsertinsert a link
:MepOrgLinkFollowfollow the link under the cursor
:MepOrgStoreLinkremember this location as a link target

The pair to learn is StoreLink then LinkInsert: store a link where you are, go elsewhere, insert it. That is how you build a link to a specific heading without typing its path.

Link syntax is org's own:

[[file:notes.org][Notes]]
[[https://example.com][A site]]
[[file:notes.org::*Some heading][That heading]]

Press f in a rendered org buffer to label every visible link and follow one by typing its letter.

Footnotes

:MepOrgFootnoteJump moves between a footnote reference and its definition, in either direction.

Citations

CommandDoes
:MepOrgBibInsertCitationinsert a citation
:MepOrgBibCiteGotojump to the entry it refers to
:MepOrgBibCitePreviewpreview that entry

Entries are read from your BibTeX files; mep.org_bib_parse_files is the parser behind it.
