Note-taking: snippets and highlights

Math autosnippets

Inside a math zone ($...$, \(...\), \[...\], or a math environment), LaTeX shortcuts expand the instant they are typed -- no key needed. In ordinary prose they stay plain text, so typing "sum" in a sentence is safe; a blank line or a heading ends any $ or \( left open, so one stray dollar can't turn the rest of the file into math. Toggle them with :MepAutosnippetsToggle; one expansion is one undo step, so u restores exactly what you typed.

Openers work from prose and create the math zone itself:

text Copy
mk       $...$ inline math
dk       \( ... \) on its own lines (inline on export)
ck       \[ ... \] on its own lines (centered on export)
flalign  full-width two-row flalign* scaffold

A sample of the in-math triggers (the full set of ~160 lives in mep.autosnippets, and the <leader>? cheatsheet lists every one):

text Copy
//  frac   \frac{num}{den}          sq       \sqrt{x}
^^         ^{exp}                   __       _{idx}
x1 x12     auto-subscript x_{12}    sum prod \sum / \prod with limits
int        \int_{a}^{b} f(x) dx     lim      \lim_{n \to \infty}
bar hat vec  accents                tt txt   \text{...}
cal bf rm bb mathcal/bf/rm/bb       ->  =>   \to  \implies
RR ZZ NN QQ CC  blackboard sets     ;a ;b ;g ;ph ;ps  Greek letters
Ev Var Cov norm  statistics         Pois Bin Unif Bern  distributions
ali aeq cases bmat pmat cvec        environments
beg        \begin{env} ... \end{env}, the end name copied as you type

Triggers fire only at the start of a word (nsum is not n + sum), except the symbolic ones (//, ->, ;a), which fire after anything. A trigger that starts with another one still works: in becomes \in as you type it, and the t of int turns that into the integral -- with one backslash. :MepAutosnippetAudit lists every such pair.

Fields and Tab

An expansion puts the cursor on its first field with the placeholder selected: type to replace it, or press Tab to keep it (lim Tab Tab Tab keeps n \to \infty and a_n). Tab walks the fields, and from the last one jumps past the closing brace, so // a Tab b Tab leaves you after \frac{a}{b}, ready for the next term. Shift-Tab goes back. Snippets nest: a // typed in a numerator is finished first, then Tab carries on in the outer one. Outside a snippet Tab is two spaces.

Org snippets (from the completion menu)

Structural templates never expand on their own -- quote, date and title are ordinary words. They show in the completion menu from the first letter; Ctrl-N / Ctrl-P move, Ctrl-Y (or Enter on an entry you moved onto) expands. Tab never touches the menu in a note, so it is always free for the fields. <leader>yy picks one from a list.

text Copy
src nsrc pysrc rsrc javasrc cppsrc   code blocks (named / per-language)
ex quote callout pcode pcodex        example, quote, labelled aside, pseudocode
title date                           note header / today's date
img pdf link cid oref sref aref      links, anchors, cross-references
tbl cite refs                        table, citation, bibliography
h1 h2 h3                             headings
mm dm cm                             math openers (menu twins of mk/dk/ck)
eqn eqref                            numbered equation and its reference
cbl                                  orange COME BACK LATER marker (prose only)

src, nsrc and callout start on a choice: Ctrl-J / Ctrl-K cycle the language or the label in place.

Typing \ plus two letters offers LaTeX commands, matched fuzzily (\lra finds \leftrightarrow); right after a \\ row break the list stays shut.

Graphs

graph, gex, gnode, gedge and gwedge insert TikZ graphs laid out by pgf's graphdrawing engine (in org and .tex buffers; the PDF needs LuaLaTeX). mgraph, mgex, mgnode, mgedge and mgwedge are the same diagrams as mermaid source blocks: HTML export renders them with mermaid.js, PDF export keeps the source as a code box.

Checking delimiters

A math zone left open only shows up later, as a PDF that will not build. Org and LaTeX buffers are checked when you save, leave Insert mode, or pause after an edit: an unclosed or mismatched $, $$, \(, \[ or math environment is marked with a warning at the delimiter itself. :MepMathLint lists them. Source blocks, #+ lines and comments are skipped; parentheses are not checked.

The cheatsheet popup

<leader>? (or :MepSnippetHelp) opens a tabbed popup documenting every trigger, symbol and note-taking key: press 1-=9=, h=/=l or Tab to switch tabs, / to fuzzy-search every row, and Enter on a row to insert that symbol -- or expand that snippet -- at the spot you opened it from.

Highlights and markup

Select text in Visual mode, then:

  • <leader>mb / <leader>mi / <leader>mm -- wrap in bold / italic / inline math.
  • <leader>mh + r o y g c b p -- highlight the selection red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue or purple.
  • <leader>mhx -- remove highlighting (selection, or under the cursor).
  • <leader>mht -- toggle the raw highlight markup view.

Highlights are stored as a plain org macro, colored live in the editor (the markup is concealed everywhere except the line you are editing) and on both HTML and PDF export:

text Copy
{{{hl(green,the highlighted words)}}}

Re-coloring part of an existing highlight splits it and keeps the rest; highlights may span lines.

Export and preview

  • <leader>oep -- export the buffer to PDF and open it in a split. While that preview is open, saving the buffer re-exports in the background and the pane refreshes itself; close the pane to stop.
  • :MepOrgExportPdf still exports without opening a viewer.

Spelling

The spell tools live under <leader>z (fix, suggestions, add word, jump). <leader>zd removes consecutive duplicate words ("the the") in the Visual selection's lines, or on the current line.
