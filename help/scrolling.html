Scrolling and the view

Scrolling

KeyScrolls
Ctrl-Ddown half a screen
Ctrl-Uup half a screen
Ctrl-Fforward a full screen
Ctrl-Bback a full screen

The cursor travels with the view, so these move you through the file rather than just moving the window over it.

Repositioning without moving

These scroll the window so that the cursor's line sits somewhere particular, leaving the cursor exactly where it is:

KeyPuts the cursor's line
zzin the middle of the pane
ztat the top
zbat the bottom

zz after a jump is the habit worth forming: it gives you context above and below whatever you just landed on.

Folds

The same z prefix opens and closes folds.

KeyDoes
zatoggle the fold at the cursor
zoopen it
zcclose it
zRopen every fold in the buffer
zMclose every fold
zrone fold level less
zmone fold level more

Folds come from the buffer's structure -- Treesitter for code, headline depth for Org and Markdown, and :MepSyntaxFold on demand. The fold marker appears in the gutter column beside the line number.

Numbers in the gutter

Line numbers are on by default in the hybrid style: the cursor's own line shows its real number, left-aligned, and every other row shows its distance from the cursor. That makes 8j or d5k a matter of reading the number rather than counting.

CommandEffect
:set numberabsolute numbers
:set relativenumberdistances
:set nonumber norelativenumberno numbers at all

The same gutter column also carries fold markers and any git, diagnostic, breakpoint or TODO sign for that line -- one mark per row, highest priority winning -- whether or not either number option is on.

Wrapping

Soft wrap is on by default: a line too wide for the pane continues on extra visual rows instead of running off the edge. :set nowrap turns it off.

A soft-wrapped line is still one buffer line. It gets one line number, and j and k move past the whole thing in one press.

Changing numbers in the text

Ctrl-A increments the next number on the line, Ctrl-X decrements it. Both take a count, so 5 Ctrl-A adds five. Leading-zero padding is preserved, so 007 becomes 008 rather than 8.

Font size

Ctrl+Shift+= and Ctrl+Shift+- grow and shrink the font, in any mode.
