* Opening a sketch

=:SketchNew= opens an empty sketch. It is the front end of mep's CAD
modeler, which is a separate thing from the polygonal 3D modeler: that one
edits vertices, this one edits /what must be true/ of the geometry and
lets a solver work out where it goes.

* The idea

A sketch is not a drawing. Place things roughly, then say what has to hold
-- these two edges are equal, this line is horizontal, that hole is 2.5
from the left -- and the solver moves everything to satisfy it at once.

The consequence worth internalising: *a dimension is an input, not a
measurement*. Changing one and re-solving is how a design is changed.

The status line along the bottom of the pane always shows where the sketch
stands:

- =solved= -- every constraint satisfied and nothing left free. What a
  finished sketch should be.
- =under_constrained= -- satisfied, but still movable, with the number of
  degrees of freedom. Not an error; most sketches are here most of the
  time.
- =redundant= -- constraints that depend on one another but agree. The
  sketch still solves; the extra ones simply say nothing new.
- =conflicting= -- constraints that depend on one another and disagree.
  Nothing satisfies them. Remove one.

Those last two look the same in most tools and are not the same problem.

* Drawing

Digits pick the tool:

| =1= | select |
| =2= | point |
| =3= | line (chains from the last point) |
| =4= | rectangle (two corners) |
| =5= | circle (centre, then a point on the rim) |
| =6= | arc (centre, start, end) |

Click to place. =Escape= abandons a half-drawn shape; a second =Escape=
clears the selection. Middle-drag pans, the wheel zooms, =F= fits the view
to what is there.

Prefer the rectangle tool to four separate lines: its corners are /shared
points/, so dragging one moves both edges that meet there and no
coincidence constraints are needed.

* Constraining

Select geometry, then press a letter. Each is the first letter of what it
does.

| =c= | coincident | two points |
| =h= | horizontal | two points, or a line |
| =v= | vertical | two points, or a line |
| =p= | parallel | two lines |
| =l= | perpendicular | two lines |
| =t= | tangent | a line and a circle/arc, or two circles/arcs |
| =e= | equal | two lines, or two circles/arcs |
| =o= | concentric | two circles/arcs |
| =n= | collinear | two lines |
| =y= | symmetric | two points and a line |
| =g= | point on object | a point and an entity |

Dimensions open a prompt, pre-filled with what the selection currently
measures -- so applying one without editing it changes nothing:

| =d= | distance | =x= | horizontal distance |
| =z= | vertical distance | =a= | angle (radians) |
| =r= | radius | =m= | diameter |

A constraint that does not suit the selection is refused and says so,
rather than quietly failing to appear.

* Dragging

Dragging a point moves it /along the directions the sketch is still free
to move in/. A dimensioned width will not budge, and the corner slides
vertically instead of the whole drag being refused.

* Anchors and construction geometry

Every sketch has a fixed origin point, drawn as a square, so that it cannot
drift. =f= anchors or releases other selected points.

Construction geometry -- a centreline, a circle used only to keep three
points concentric -- is drawn dashed and is ignored when profiles are
found. =k= toggles it on the selection; =K= places new geometry as
construction until pressed again.

* Profiles

The closed regions the non-construction geometry bounds are marked with
their area. Nested regions are reported as /holes/ of the region around
them, not as separate profiles: a washer drawn as two circles is one
profile with one hole, which is the distinction an extrude needs.

Areas are integrated along the real curves, so a circle's area is its
area and not that of the polygon it is drawn with.

* Driving it from a script or an agent

Every key above ends in the same call that =mep.sketch_*= (Lua) and
=sketch.*= (agent RPC) make, so anything that can be done by hand can be
done headlessly, and the two cannot drift apart. See =MEP_AGENT_API.md=.
