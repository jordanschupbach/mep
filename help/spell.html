Spell checking

The squiggle

Misspelled words get a red underline, drawn through the same decoration pipeline as syntax and diagnostics.

The keys

Everything is on the <leader>z group, and it works in Visual mode too.

KeyDoes
<leader>zttoggle spell checking
<leader>zssuggestions for the word, in a picker
<leader>zffix the word with the best suggestion
<leader>znnext misspelled word
<leader>zpprevious misspelled word
<leader>zgadd the word to your dictionary
<leader>zwmark the word wrong

<leader>zf is the fast path when the intended word is obvious; zs is for when it is not.

Your dictionary

<leader>zg adds a word permanently, so a project's jargon stops being flagged. <leader>zw does the opposite, marking a word wrong even though the dictionary accepts it.

From Lua

lua Copy
mep.spell_bad(word)        --> is it misspelled?
mep.spell_suggest(word)    --> an array of suggestions
mep.spell_add(word)        --  add to the personal dictionary
mep.spell_wrong(word)      --  mark it wrong
mep.spell_ready()          --> is the dictionary loaded yet?

The checker is a dependency-free C++ implementation, so there is no hunspell or aspell to install.
