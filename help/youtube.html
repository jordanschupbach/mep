YouTube player

What it is

A pane that searches YouTube, lists what it finds and plays the video you pick -- picture and sound, drawn by mep itself, beside whatever you are editing. Nothing is embedded: yt-dlp resolves the video to its stream URLs and ffmpeg decodes them into raw frames and samples over a pipe, and the pane paints those. Both tools must be on PATH; the pane says so if one is missing.

Opening it

  • <leader>yt toggles the player: the first press opens it in a vertical split next to the current pane, the next hides it again (so does i inside the pane). Hiding does not stop anything -- the video plays on unseen, sound and all, and the press after that brings the same pane back mid-video. The YouTube icon at the right end of the tab bar does the same thing on a click; it is lit while the player is on screen, and its tooltip says when one is playing in the background.
  • :youtube (or :yt) opens the player in the current pane. Give it a search: :youtube lofi hip hop. Give it a watch URL, a youtu.be link or a bare 11-character video id and it plays that directly.
  • :MepYoutube is the command behind the toggle, for a keybinding of your own. From Lua: mep.youtube_toggle(), mep.youtube_search(query), mep.youtube_play(url) and mep.youtube_state() (whose visible field is false while the player is hidden).

There is one player per workspace; opening it again brings the existing one back instead of starting a second. To actually stop playback, press x (stop, keep the pane) or q (close the pane and stop) in the pane, or :bd its buffer.

The status bar pill

Whenever a track is loaded, a pill joins the chips at the right end of the status bar, left of direnv: a play/pause icon, the elapsed/total readout, and the title scrolling past in a fixed window so a long one is readable without the pill growing to fit it. It is red while the track is running and gray while it is paused or finished, clicking it pauses or resumes, and it is there whether the player pane is on screen or hidden -- which is how a player left playing in the background says what it is playing. It disappears when nothing is loaded.

Changing track -- n=/=p, the transport's prev/next buttons, or the list playing on by itself at the end of a video -- also sends a Now playing: notification with the title and channel, so a background player announces itself without the pane being open. <leader>nn (the notifications pane) has them all.

How playback works

The audio track is streamed to the sound device and the video track is timed against it: a frame is shown when the sound has reached its timestamp, so picture and sound stay together, and dropped when the picture falls behind. Without a sound device (or for a video with no audio) the wall clock drives the picture instead.

Seeking restarts the decoders at the new position, so it takes a moment -- the pane says Buffering... while it waits for the first frame. Quality is capped at 480p, which is what fits a pane and keeps the decoder cheap.

When it does not work

  • yt-dlp and ffmpeg must be installed: the tools are looked up on PATH when the pane opens.
  • A 403 or a blank search usually means yt-dlp is out of date; YouTube changes often and yt-dlp follows. Update it and try again.
  • The player asks yt-dlp for the android client's stream URLs, which ffmpeg can fetch directly; other clients hand out URLs that only yt-dlp itself may fetch.
