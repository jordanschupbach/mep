* What it is

A pane that searches YouTube, lists what it finds and plays the video you
pick -- picture and sound, drawn by mep itself, beside whatever you are
editing. Nothing is embedded: =yt-dlp= resolves the video to its stream
URLs and =ffmpeg= decodes them into raw frames and samples over a pipe, and
the pane paints those. Both tools must be on =PATH=; the pane says so if
one is missing.

* Opening it

- =<leader>yt= toggles the player: the first press opens it in a vertical
  split next to the current pane, the next hides it again (so does =i=
  inside the pane). Hiding does not stop anything -- the video plays on
  unseen, sound and all, and the press after that brings the same pane
  back mid-video. The YouTube icon
  at the right end of the tab bar does the same thing on a click; it is
  lit while the player is on screen, and its tooltip says when one is
  playing in the background.
- =:youtube= (or =:yt=) opens the player in the current pane. Give it a
  search: =:youtube lofi hip hop=. Give it a watch URL, a =youtu.be= link
  or a bare 11-character video id and it plays that directly.
- =:MepYoutube= is the command behind the toggle, for a keybinding of
  your own. From Lua: =mep.youtube_toggle()=, =mep.youtube_search(query)=,
  =mep.youtube_play(url)= and =mep.youtube_state()= (whose =visible= field
  is false while the player is hidden).

There is one player per workspace; opening it again brings the existing
one back instead of starting a second. To actually stop playback, press
=x= (stop, keep the pane) or =q= (close the pane and stop) in the pane,
or =:bd= its buffer.

* Keys in the pane

=?= in the pane shows this same list as a sheet over the video -- the
=?: help= line along the pane's bottom edge is the reminder that it is
there, exactly like a sidebar's. =Esc= (or a click) puts it away.

| Key              | Does                                                 |
|------------------+------------------------------------------------------|
| =?=              | show the key sheet over the player (=Esc= closes it) |
| =/= or =s=       | search (a URL typed here plays instead of searching) |
| =o=              | open a watch URL or video id                         |
| =j= / =k=        | move through the results (=g= / =G= top / bottom)    |
| =Enter=          | play the selected result; on the one already playing, pause / resume |
| =C-Space=        | pause / resume -- works whatever your leader key is  |
| =Space=          | pause / resume -- unless Space is your leader key (the default), when it opens the leader menu instead and =C-Space= / =Enter= are the pause |
| =i=              | hide the pane and keep playing (=<leader>yt= brings it back) |
| =h= / =l=        | volume down / up                                     |
| =H= / =L=        | seek 10 seconds back / forward                       |
| =Left= / =Right= | seek 10 seconds back / forward (with =Shift=: a minute) |
| =0= ... =9=      | jump to 0%, 10%, ... 90% of the video                |
| =C-h= / =C-l=    | previous / next result (=C-p= / =C-n= do the same)   |
| =n= / =p=        | next / previous result (the list plays on by itself at the end of a video) |
| =+= / =-=        | volume                                               |
| =m=              | mute                                                 |
| =x=              | stop                                                 |
| =r=              | hide or show the result list (the video takes the whole pane) |
| =q=              | close the pane and stop (=<leader>yt= hides it but keeps playing) |

=:= and the leader key work as everywhere else, so =<leader>yt= hides the
player from inside it too. The mouse does the same things: click a result
to play it, the picture to pause, the progress bar to seek, the speaker to
mute, and drag the volume slider.

* The status bar pill

Whenever a track is loaded, a pill joins the chips at the right end of the
status bar, left of =direnv=: a play/pause icon, the elapsed/total readout,
and the title scrolling past in a fixed window so a long one is readable
without the pill growing to fit it. It is red while the track is running
and gray while it is paused or finished, clicking it pauses or resumes,
and it is there whether the player pane is on screen or hidden -- which is
how a player left playing in the background says what it is playing. It
disappears when nothing is loaded.

Changing track -- =n=/=p=, the transport's prev/next buttons, or the list
playing on by itself at the end of a video -- also sends a /Now playing:/
notification with the title and channel, so a background player announces
itself without the pane being open. =<leader>nn= (the notifications pane)
has them all.

* How playback works

The audio track is streamed to the sound device and the video track is
timed against it: a frame is shown when the sound has reached its
timestamp, so picture and sound stay together, and dropped when the
picture falls behind. Without a sound device (or for a video with no
audio) the wall clock drives the picture instead.

Seeking restarts the decoders at the new position, so it takes a moment --
the pane says /Buffering.../ while it waits for the first frame. Quality
is capped at 480p, which is what fits a pane and keeps the decoder cheap.

* When it does not work

- /yt-dlp and ffmpeg must be installed/: the tools are looked up on =PATH=
  when the pane opens.
- A 403 or a blank search usually means =yt-dlp= is out of date; YouTube
  changes often and =yt-dlp= follows. Update it and try again.
- The player asks =yt-dlp= for the =android= client's stream URLs, which
  =ffmpeg= can fetch directly; other clients hand out URLs that only
  =yt-dlp= itself may fetch.
