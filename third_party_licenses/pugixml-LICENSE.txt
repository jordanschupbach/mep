pugixml v1.16 -- https://pugixml.org/ / https://github.com/zeux/pugixml
Fetched via CMake FetchContent (see CMakeLists.txt's `pugixml` block) for
DOCX/ODT XML parsing in src/office_doc.cpp (both formats are ZIP archives
of XML parts). Not git-committed source -- built from the upstream
release tarball at configure time, the same way raylib/lua/miniz are.
